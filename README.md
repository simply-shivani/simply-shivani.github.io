# Shivani K. — Portfolio & Career Paths

The home page has seven tabs. About combines Shivani's story, academics, activities and achievements in one place. Career & Programs contains a general interactive Ontario career planning guide, including all eight career branches, courses, routes, licensing, supplemental applications, graduation guidance and outside-credit guidance. All site files are contained in this folder.

Open `index.html` directly, or serve this folder with `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit `http://localhost:8000`. The site uses plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and can be hosted directly on GitHub Pages.

- `index.html`: profile content and the shared page layout.
- The About story uses a “Shivani K” name card in place of a portrait.
- `styles.css`: portfolio styling and scoped career guide styling.
- `school-guides.js`: data and rendering for the **PDSB Schools** and **DPCDSB Schools** tabs (rankings, special programs, open house dates, best schools by career area). Edit the `PDSB`, `PDSB_EV`, `DP` and `DP_EV` lists at the top to update schools or dates; dates are compared with the visitor's current day to mark sessions Upcoming or Past.
- `ontario-rankings.js`: the **Ontario Rankings** tab, listing all 747 schools from the Fraser Institute 2025 report (`index.html#ontario`). Peel schools are tagged `~P` (PDSB) or `~D` (DPCDSB) in the data.
- `university-rankings.js`: the **University Rankings** tab (`index.html#universities`): Maclean’s 2027 large and mid-size rankings with tuition, median entering grades and all 15 ranking measures, plus a Grade 9–12 application timeline paraphrased from Maclean’s “How to Apply” guide. Small universities link out to Maclean’s.
- `world-rankings.js` and `world-rankings.css`: the **World Top 100** section inside the University Rankings tab (`index.html#universities`; its markup sits in `index.html` and `university-rankings.js` moves it into the tab), adapted from “Top 100 for Canadians”: the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2027 top 100 with tuition for Canadian citizens (converted to CAD at editable placeholder exchange rates), how well the OSSD, AP and IB are accepted at each school, US credit policies, UK IB offers, country rules, language of teaching (`LANG_C` by country, `LANG_U` per-school overrides) and sources. Its sub-tabs are Overview, Universities, Costs, IB and AP, and Method and sources. The `U` list holds the schools, `RATES0` the exchange rates, and `SOURCES` the links. All styles are scoped to `#guide-world`; its markup lives in `index.html`.
- `career-paths.js`: the **Career & Programs** tab (`index.html#career`). It has a Home tab and eight branch tabs, and each branch shows a tree of Branch → Career → University program → Postgrad options. For each career it covers the route, licences, supplemental applications, courses by grade, outside-credit guidance and the 30-credit plan; for each program it covers what you would study, postgrad options, work after postgrad and what happens if you stop after undergrad. Edit the `C` (careers), `PD` (programs) and `PG` (postgrad labels in the tree) data to update content. A destination-career view is planned later.
- `pathways.js`: the **Pathways & Schools** tab (`index.html#pathways`). Pick one of the eight career pathways to see the best-fit academic programs (Regular, regional AP, local AP, ELP, SciTech, IBT, IB, Regional Arts, Transportation Engineering & Technology, and the DPCDSB AP, IB, STEM/STEAM and Arts programs), then career experience (EHS, SHSM, co-op). Each option shows what it takes to get in (exam, essay or written answers, video, audition, marks only, or just register), whether a random draw applies, the PDSB and DPCDSB schools with Fraser ranks and matching SHSM/EHS tags, the entry steps, dates and costs. Filters: school board and entry requirement. A table at the bottom lists every program and how to get in. Edit `O` (programs and entry rules) and `P` (pathways, fit ratings and reasons). School ranks and SHSM/EHS sectors are read from `school-guides.js` through `window.PEEL_SCHOOLS`, so update schools there. PDSB rules are from the 2027–28 Regional Learning Choices and Explore High Skills pages and the EHS FAQ, checked 15 Nov 2026; DPCDSB rules are from policy P-2007. The EHS and DPCDSB AP written answers were reported by families and are not on the board pages.
- `portfolio.js`: portfolio navigation, keyboard support and section URL hashes. Any in-page link to a section (for example `#pathways`) switches tabs.

Career & Programs is also available directly through `index.html#career`. Switching portfolio tabs retains the selected career branch. Older `#journey` and `#achievements` links fall back to About.

Career & Programs Home includes a program comparison of Regular, AP, ELP, SciTech, EHS, SHSM, IBT and IB (grade timeline, PDSB and DPCDSB schools, entry routes; details from PDSB 2027–28 entry pages and DPCDSB policy P-2007), then a “Best approach and schools by career” card that links each branch to the Pathways & Schools tab, followed by “Regular vs AP Course Comparison”. It covers Grade 9 English, mathematics and science, local course-code suffixes, assessment, pathway planning and family support. Subject comparisons expand individually; enrichment examples must be confirmed with the school.

The public profile uses generalized activity venues and academic tiles showing subject strengths and scores. Career & Programs uses general examples rather than a personal school plan. It is an unofficial planning guide: confirm current requirements with your school and intended postsecondary programs. The visible “Last updated” date records the latest content edit, not a verification date for every policy, fee or deadline.

PDSB Schools and DPCDSB Schools are also available at `index.html#pdsb` and `index.html#dpcdsb`. Ranks are from the Fraser Institute 2025 Report Card (2023/24 results) and cover only literacy and Grade 9 math tests. “Best by career” is a shortlist: the highest-ranked schools that announced a matching SHSM, EHS or regional program. It is not an official ranking. Open house details were taken from each board's public pages on 18 October 2026 and can change.
