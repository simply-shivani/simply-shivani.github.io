# Shivani K. — Portfolio & Career Paths

The home page has six tabs. About combines Shivani's story, academics, activities and achievements in one place. Career & Programs contains a general interactive Ontario career planning guide, including all eight career branches, courses, routes, licensing, supplemental applications, graduation guidance and outside-credit guidance. All site files are contained in this folder.

Open `index.html` directly, or serve this folder with `python3 -m http.server 8000` and visit `http://localhost:8000`. The site uses plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and can be hosted directly on GitHub Pages.

- `index.html`: profile content and the shared page layout.
- The About story uses a “Shivani K” name card in place of a portrait.
- `styles.css`: portfolio styling and scoped career guide styling.
- `school-guides.js`: data and rendering for the **PDSB Schools** and **DPCDSB Schools** tabs (rankings, special programs, open house dates, best schools by career area). Edit the `PDSB`, `PDSB_EV`, `DP` and `DP_EV` lists at the top to update schools or dates; dates are compared with the visitor's current day to mark sessions Upcoming or Past.
- `ontario-rankings.js`: the **Ontario Rankings** tab, listing all 747 schools from the Fraser Institute 2025 report (`index.html#ontario`). Peel schools are tagged `~P` (PDSB) or `~D` (DPCDSB) in the data.
- `university-rankings.js`: the **University Rankings** tab (`index.html#universities`): Maclean’s 2027 large and mid-size rankings with tuition, median entering grades and all 15 ranking measures, plus a Grade 9–12 application timeline paraphrased from Maclean’s “How to Apply” guide. Small universities link out to Maclean’s.
- `world-rankings.js` and `world-rankings.css`: the **World Top 100** section inside the University Rankings tab (`index.html#universities`; its markup sits in `index.html` and `university-rankings.js` moves it into the tab), adapted from “Top 100 for Canadians”: the Times Higher Education World University Rankings 2027 top 100 with tuition for Canadian citizens (converted to CAD at editable placeholder exchange rates), how well the OSSD, AP and IB are accepted at each school, US credit policies, UK IB offers, country rules and sources. Its sub-tabs are Overview, Universities, Costs, IB and AP, and Method and sources. The `U` list holds the schools, `RATES0` the exchange rates, and `SOURCES` the links. All styles are scoped to `#guide-world`; its markup lives in `index.html`.
- `career-paths.js`: the **Career & Programs** tab (`index.html#career`). It has a Home tab and eight branch tabs, and each branch shows a tree of Branch → Career → University program → Postgrad options. For each career it covers the route, licences, supplemental applications, courses by grade, outside-credit guidance and the 30-credit plan; for each program it covers what you would study, postgrad options, work after postgrad and what happens if you stop after undergrad. Edit the `C` (careers), `PD` (programs) and `PG` (postgrad labels in the tree) data to update content. A destination-career view is planned later.
- `portfolio.js`: portfolio navigation, keyboard support and section URL hashes.

Career & Programs is also available directly through `index.html#career`. Switching portfolio tabs retains the selected career branch. Older `#journey` and `#achievements` links fall back to About.

Career & Programs Home includes “Regular vs AP Course Comparison” directly after the program comparison. It covers Grade 9 English, mathematics and science, local course-code suffixes, assessment, pathway planning and family support. Subject comparisons expand individually; enrichment examples must be confirmed with the school.

The public profile uses generalized activity venues and academic tiles showing subject strengths and scores. Career & Programs uses general examples rather than a personal school plan. It is an unofficial planning guide: confirm current requirements with your school and intended postsecondary programs. The visible “Last updated” date records the latest content edit, not a verification date for every policy, fee or deadline.

PDSB Schools and DPCDSB Schools are also available at `index.html#pdsb` and `index.html#dpcdsb`. Ranks are from the Fraser Institute 2025 Report Card (2023/24 results) and cover only literacy and Grade 9 math tests. “Best by career” is a shortlist: the highest-ranked schools that announced a matching SHSM, EHS or regional program. It is not an official ranking. Open house details were taken from each board's public pages on 18 October 2026 and can change.
